# Code of Conduct

We work with people from different countries, cultures and time zones.
Everyone participating in 4 Digital Asset repositories is expected to:

- Be respectful and professional in code reviews, issues and discussions.
- Critique the code, not the person. Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Assume good intent and remember that English may not be everyone's first language.
- Respect confidentiality: client information and private code stay private.
- Not tolerate harassment, discrimination or offensive language of any kind.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **4digitalasset@gmail.com**. All reports are
handled confidentially.

## Consequences

Organization owners may remove comments, revoke repository access or remove
participants who do not follow this code of conduct.
